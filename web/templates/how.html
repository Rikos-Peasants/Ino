How it works

Three separate systems, often confused for each other. This is what each one actually does.

Scoring

Post an image in one of the art channels and it enters the leaderboard automatically. There is no command and no submission form.

A 👍 adds a point. A 👎 removes one. Your score is the net across everything you have ever posted, which is why someone with four thousand images can sit below someone with one thousand. Volume is not the same as being good.

Rankings recalculate the moment a reaction lands. Nothing resets weekly, and there is no appeals process.

Bot reactions are subtracted, so Ino adding a 👍 to seed a post does not inflate anyone.

InoRep

Separate from your art score, and unrelated to it. InoRep is how Ino feels about you personally, and moderators adjust it by hand.

Being helpful moves it up. Being warned moves it down. It runs from deeply negative to genuinely absurd, with a named tier at every step, and you can read yours on your page once you have logged in with Discord.

Logging in reads only your Discord username and avatar. Nothing is posted on your behalf and the access token is discarded once your profile has been read.

The donation goal

One goal runs at a time. Every Ko-fi donation counts toward it the second Ko-fi confirms the payment, and the bar on the donations page is the live figure rather than a number someone updates by hand.

Tick the private box on Ko-fi and only the total moves. Your name and message stay off the page entirely, but the money still counts.

Donations in other currencies convert to USD before they are added, so the total is comparable regardless of where you are.

What we keep

Your name, your message, the amount, and whether you asked to stay private. That is the whole list.

Ko-fi also sends us an email address, and a postal address on shop orders. Neither is written to the database or passed to Discord. The email is used once, to send you a thank-you, and then it is gone.
